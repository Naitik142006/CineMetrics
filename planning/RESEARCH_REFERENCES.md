## 1. Academic Peer-Reviewed Literature

### Machine Learning Algorithms & Explainability
1. **CatBoost (Ordered Boosting)**:
   - Prokhorenkova, L., Gusev, G., Vorobev, A., Dorogush, A. V., & Gulin, A. (2018). *CatBoost: unbiased boosting with categorical features*. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2018), 31.
   - [arXiv:1706.09516](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.09516)
2. **SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) & TreeSHAP**:
   - Lundberg, S. M., & Lee, S. I. (2017). *A unified approach to interpreting model predictions*. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2017), 30.
   - Lundberg, S. M., et al. (2020). *From local explanations to global understanding with explainable AI for trees*. Nature Machine Intelligence, 2(1), 56-67.
   - [Nature Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-019-0138-9) | [SHAP GitHub](https://github.com/shap/shap)
3. **Conformal Prediction & Uncertainty Quantification**:
   - Vovk, V., Gammerman, A., & Shafer, G. (2005). *Algorithmic Learning in a Random World*. Springer Science & Business Media.
   - Angelopoulos, A. N., & Bates, S. (2021). *A Gentle Introduction to Conformal Prediction and Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification*. arXiv preprint arXiv:2107.07511.
   - [arXiv:2107.07511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07511)
4. **Multilingual NLP for Indian Languages**:
   - Khanuja, S., et al. (2021). *MuRIL: Multilingual Representations for Indian Languages*. Google Research India. arXiv preprint arXiv:2103.10730.
   - [HuggingFace MuRIL Model](https://huggingface.co/google/muril-base-cased) | [arXiv:2103.10730](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10730)

### Box-Office Forecasting & Entertainment Economics
5. **Cinema Econometrics & Word-of-Mouth**:
   - De Vany, A., & Walls, W. D. (1996). *Bose-Einstein dynamics and adaptive contracting in the motion picture industry*. The Economic Journal, 106(439), 1493-1514.
   - Eliashberg, J., Elberse, A., & Leenders, M. A. (2006). *The motion picture industry: Critical issues in practice, current research, and new research directions*. Marketing Science, 25(6), 638-661.
6. **Machine Learning for Movie Revenue Prediction**:
   - Asur, S., & Huberman, B. A. (2010). *Predicting the future with social media*. 2010 IEEE/WIC/ACM International Conference on Web Intelligence, 492-499.
   - Kim, T., et al. (2015). *Box-office forecasting based on social media analysis: A systematic literature review*. Telematics and Informatics, 32(4), 723-739.
